1 · AI and our people

AI lets us produce more. But are we still growing?

Our position?

AI should help colleagues work faster with the right guidance.

Managers must spend the time saved on their development, not on more output.

1

The risk

Team Europe colleagues pigeonholed into producing client content with AI lose the habit of interrogating evidence and questioning the task.

2

The choice

Do we make them “content producers” who drive AI tools, or future leaders who work faster with guidance?

3

The tools

Leaders can review faster too: for example, running a colleague's work through ChatGPT to see how they are developing.

8 of 13in our survey disagree that AI helps them learn and build skills, including all three analysts.

3 · Trying harder

Some of the most interesting AI applications are complex; persistence beats talent

“Try Harder”

OffSec's motto for security learners: when you're stuck, research, retry and work it out for yourself.

Lessons from cyber security

Digital systems are complex; you only get in by testing, failing and digging deeper.

Other tech work

Code, data and platforms are often more complicated than they need to be. Being cautious about what we build, and perseverance, are how you understand them.

Consulting and advisory

The same habit we bring to an unfamiliar client problem: keep digging until it makes sense.

Build the mental model, not the code: IBM Full Stack Software Developer · Python for Everybody (University of Michigan) · both on Coursera

4 · What we've built

How these tools were built, and what it costs

My own device

Claude Code runs inside VS Code on my personal laptop, not a firm device.

Approved tools first

ChatGPT is approved in the firm; other AI tools are not yet, though a trial may follow. The case studies use public, non-confidential data only.

IT permissions

Setting this up on firm devices needs individual permissions from the IT team, which is hard at the moment.

It costs money

Claude Code is a paid tool. I once (accidentally) spent $200 building the trade tool in a single 5-hour session.

Case study B · Automating work

Case study B: a rapid analysis in under two hours

Ideation (1h)Work together to identify a business opportunity: explain the question, process, output and success criteria
Instructions (10 min)Present the idea to the AI to build a decisions file, then an instructions file for the build
Build (60 min)The AI agent builds the data, tool or analysis per the instructions. Returns an alpha model
ProductReview, assess, amend: the lifecycle finishes when staff are happy
